WHAT TO LOOK FOR
01
Human proportions

Softer head, shorter neck, shoulders and arms instead of square blocks.

02
Proper rowing mechanics

Both hands move with the oar grips, while each oar rotates through a continuous stroke at its mount.

03
Water that doesn't tile

Layered surface normals and subtle motion, without exposed diamond-shaped mesh cells.

04
A Dhaka setting

Balconies, cables, rooftop tanks, colorful shops and floodwater as environmental direction.
