What We Are Testing

  1. Adult Character Proportions

    Natural head size, shoulders, arms, neck, clothing, and seated posture.

  2. Connected Hands

    Both hands maintain contact with the paddle grips during rowing.

  3. Real Rowing Cycle

    Catch, drive, release, and recovery with natural diagonal oar movement.

  4. Believable Boat

    Curved wooden hull, raised sides, visible seating, and natural proportions.
